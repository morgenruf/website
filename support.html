Frequently Asked Questions

How do I install Morgenruf in my Slack workspace?
  1. Click the Add to Slack button on morgenruf.dev.
  2. Authorise Morgenruf in your Slack workspace (you need the Manage Apps permission).
  3. You'll be redirected to the Morgenruf dashboard to complete setup.
  4. Choose your standup channel, time, and team members.
  5. Click Save — your team will receive their first standup DM at the scheduled time.

For self-hosted deployments, see the README for Helm/Docker instructions.

How do I change the standup schedule or questions?
  1. Go to the Morgenruf dashboard.
  2. Select your workspace and click Schedules in the sidebar.
  3. Edit the schedule — change the time, timezone, days, channel, or questions.
  4. Click Save changes. The next standup will use the new settings.

You can also create multiple schedules for different teams or channels.

How do I add or remove team members from standups?
  1. Open the dashboard → Members.
  2. To add: click Add members and select Slack users. They will receive a DM at the next standup time.
  3. To remove: find the member and click Remove. They will no longer receive standup DMs.

Members can also opt themselves out by replying /standup skip or /standup leave in Slack.

How do I export standup data?
  1. Open the dashboard → Analytics.
  2. Set the date range you want to export.
  3. Click Export CSV. A CSV file with all standup responses for the selected period will download.

The CSV includes: date, user, each question/answer, and mood rating (if enabled). You can also request a full data export via email at privacy@morgenruf.dev.

How do I uninstall Morgenruf?
  1. In Slack, go to Settings & administration → Manage apps.
  2. Find Morgenruf and click Remove app.
  3. Confirm the removal. Morgenruf will immediately stop sending standups.

All workspace data is automatically deleted within 30 days of uninstallation. For immediate deletion, email privacy@morgenruf.dev with your Slack team ID.
