How it works

Up and running in 30 seconds

No complex setup, no DevOps degree required. Install, configure, and your team is async-first.

1

Install in 30 seconds

Click "Add to Slack" and authorize Morgenruf in your workspace. One click, zero configuration needed to get started.

2

Set your standup

Choose your questions, schedule, timezone, and team members from the web dashboard. Fully customizable to your workflow.

3

Get daily updates

Morgenruf DMs each team member, collects answers, and posts a clean summary to your #standup channel automatically.

Features

Everything your team needs

Morgenruf packs every feature from paid tools — 100% free, because open source wins.

Custom Questions

Ask exactly what matters to your team. Create any questions, reorder them, and update anytime from the dashboard.

Smart Scheduling

Timezone-aware scheduling that respects working hours and weekends. Each team member gets messages at the right time.

Beautiful Reports

Grouped summaries with clear formatting posted to any channel. Your whole team gets a clean daily digest.

Skip & Remind

DM skip to opt out for the day. Configurable pre-standup reminder DMs keep everyone on time.

Webhooks

Push standup data to any endpoint. Integrate with your own tools, analytics, or automation pipelines.

Dashboard

Full web UI to manage standups, team members, schedules, and reports — all in one place.

Mood Tracking

After blockers, the bot asks how team members are feeling — great · okay · rough. Tracked in analytics over time.

Participation Analytics

Dashboard analytics tab shows per-member 7-day and 30-day participation rates, blockers count, and last standup date. Export to CSV anytime.

Per-User Timezone

Each team member can DM timezone Europe/London to set their personal timezone independently of the workspace schedule.

Weekly Digest

Automatic Sunday email to the workspace admin summarising the week — total responses, participation rate, and per-member breakdown.

Rich App Home

A beautiful Slack App Home tab showing all your standups, streaks, timezone, and quick actions — start, pause, configure, or delete in one click.

Standup Streaks

Track consecutive standup days per member. Builds healthy habits and keeps your team motivated with visible streak counts.

Vacation Mode

Click "I'm away" and the bot stops bothering you until you're back. No missed standups cluttering your inbox while you recharge.

Answer Prefill

Yesterday's answers pre-populate today's standup form, so you can quickly update what changed instead of starting from scratch.

Previous Standups

Browse your last 10 standups right from the App Home. See your answers, mood, and dates at a glance — no dashboard needed.

Channel Sync

Optionally sync standup participants with channel membership. New channel members automatically join the standup.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Morgenruf.

Is Morgenruf really free? +
Yes. Morgenruf is 100% free and open source under the MIT license. There are no paid tiers, no per-user pricing, and no credit card required. You self-host it on your own infrastructure, so the only cost is your server (as low as $5/month on most cloud providers).
How does Morgenruf compare to Geekbot? +
Morgenruf offers all the core features of Geekbot — custom questions, scheduling, Slack DM collection, channel summaries, and analytics — but is completely free and self-hosted. Geekbot charges $2.50/user/month and stores your data on their servers. With Morgenruf, you own your data and pay nothing per user.
Where is my standup data stored? +
Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database on your own infrastructure. Morgenruf is fully self-hosted — your standup responses, team data, and analytics never leave your servers. There is zero telemetry or data collection.
Can I use Morgenruf with multiple Slack workspaces? +
Yes. Morgenruf supports multi-workspace Slack OAuth out of the box. You can install it in as many Slack workspaces as you need, all managed from a single deployment.
How long does it take to set up? +
About 30 seconds for the Slack installation. Click "Add to Slack", authorize the app, and configure your standup questions from the web dashboard. For self-hosted deployment, Docker Compose, Helm charts, and AWS CloudFormation templates are all provided.
Does Morgenruf support Microsoft Teams or Google Chat? +
Google Chat support is currently in beta, and Microsoft Teams support is actively being developed. Slack is the primary and fully supported platform today.
