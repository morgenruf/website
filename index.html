Recognition

Praise that means something because it runs out

Everyone gets a handful of tokens a day to give away, and unused ones do not carry over. Scarcity is the whole mechanic: unlimited praise is worth nothing, and a daily budget is what makes people actually spend it.

  • Pick your own token and daily amount
  • Resets at midnight in each person's timezone
  • Leaderboards for giving, not just receiving

Coffee chats

Introduce the people who never talk

  • One quiet nudge if the conversation stalls
  • Asks afterwards whether you actually met

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Morgenruf.

Is Morgenruf really free? +
Yes. Morgenruf is 100% free and open source under the MIT license. There are no paid tiers, no per-user pricing, and no credit card required. You self-host it on your own infrastructure, so the only cost is your server (as low as $5/month on most cloud providers).
How does Morgenruf compare to Geekbot? +
Morgenruf offers all the core features of Geekbot — custom questions, scheduling, Slack DM collection, channel summaries, and analytics — but is completely free and self-hosted. Geekbot charges $2.50/user/month and stores your data on their servers. With Morgenruf, you own your data and pay nothing per user.
Where is my standup data stored? +
Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database on your own infrastructure. Morgenruf is fully self-hosted — your standup responses, team data, and analytics never leave your servers. There is zero telemetry or data collection.
Can I use Morgenruf with multiple Slack workspaces? +
Yes. Morgenruf supports multi-workspace Slack OAuth out of the box. You can install it in as many Slack workspaces as you need, all managed from a single deployment.
How long does it take to set up? +
About 30 seconds for the Slack installation. Click "Add to Slack", authorize the app, and configure your standup questions from the web dashboard. For self-hosted deployment, Docker Compose, Helm charts, and AWS CloudFormation templates are all provided.
Does Morgenruf support Microsoft Teams or Google Chat? +
Google Chat support is currently in beta, and Microsoft Teams support is actively being developed. Slack is the primary and fully supported platform today.
