How it works

Async standups in 4 simple steps

No meetings, no calendar invites — just structured updates that keep everyone aligned.

1

Bot sends DMs

At your scheduled time, Morgenruf DMs each team member with your custom standup questions.

2

Team responds

Members reply in Slack DMs. Takes about 2 minutes. They can also skip for the day.

3

Summary posted

A formatted summary with all responses and mood indicators is posted to your #standup channel.

4

Everyone in sync

Your whole team sees what everyone is working on, any blockers, and how the team is feeling.

Features

Everything you need for Slack standups

Feature-complete standup bot, 100% free.

🎯

Custom questions

Choose from 25 templates or write your own. Ask what matters to your team.

⏰

Smart scheduling

Timezone-aware with per-user overrides. Messages arrive at each person's local time.

📊

Channel summaries

Beautiful, formatted standup reports posted to any Slack channel automatically.

🎭

Mood tracking

Optional mood check-in with every standup. Track team health trends over time.

Participation analytics

7-day and 30-day participation rates, blocker counts, and last-standup dates.

Skip & remind

Team members can DM "skip" to opt out. Pre-standup reminders keep everyone on time.

Webhooks

HMAC-signed webhooks push standup data to any endpoint for integrations.

🏠

Web dashboard

Full UI to manage standups, members, schedules, and review historical data.

Weekly digest

Automatic weekly email to admins with participation summary and per-member breakdown.
